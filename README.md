# My Favorite K-Drama: Our Beloved Summer 🌿

## About the Series
**Our Beloved Summer** is a South Korean romantic comedy-drama starring Choi Woo-shik and Kim Da-mi. It tells the story of Choi Ung and Kook Yeon-su, two former lovers who unexpectedly reunite when a documentary they filmed during high school becomes popular again.

## Why I Like It
I like this K-drama because it has a realistic and heartwarming story. It shows how relationships can be complicated and how people grow and change over time. I also love the chemistry between the main characters and how their story develops throughout the series.

## Main Characters
- **Choi Ung** – A quiet and talented illustrator.
- **Kook Yeon-su** – An ambitious and hardworking woman.
- **Kim Ji-ung** – Their mutual friend and documentary director.
- **NJ** – A popular idol who becomes interested in Choi Ung.

## My Favorite Things About the Drama
1. The natural chemistry between the main characters.
2. The beautiful cinematography and background music.
3. The funny but emotional scenes.
4. The lessons about love, personal growth, and second chances.

## My Rating
<text>**10/10**</text>

I would definitely recommend this drama to anyone who enjoys romance, comedy, and stories that make you feel different emotions.
